## The Modpacks

There are also several ways you can support the modpack:

- ▶️ Play using the [CurseForge App](https://curseforge.com/download/app) or [Modrinth App](https://modrinth.com/app)
  - Using either of these launchers gives the pack and mod devs money, other launchers currently do not.
- 🛑 Disable ad blocker on modrinth
- ❤️ [Check out my other projects](https://henrymeyer.de/projects)
- 🔗 [Tell your frinds to use HM/JS modpacks and resource packs by sharing the link](https://gaming.henrymeyer.de/)
